Track Volume

Knob: turn to change the volume, press to mute. Key: live level, tap to mute. Pick Follow Track Switcher to control whatever the Track Switcher has picked.

Track Switcher

Knob: turn to pick an input, press to mute it. Key: tap for the next input, hold to mute. Track Volume set to Follow Track Switcher controls the picked input.

Duo Meter

Two inputs on one key. Tap mutes the top one, hold mutes the bottom one. The row lights up while its knob turns.

Mic Mute

A tap mutes or unmutes the input in OBS. When muted, the key gets a red frame and says MIC OFF.

Stream

Tap to go live. Hold for half a second to stop, so a stray tap never ends the stream.

Record

Tap to start, tap again to pause or resume. Hold to stop.

Scene

Tap switches to the scene. In Studio Mode a tap puts it in preview and a second tap sends it on air. Hold cuts straight to air.

Scene Switcher

Knob: turn to pick a scene, press to put it on air (Studio Mode: preview first, press again to go live). Key: shows the pick, tap to put it on air, hold to cut straight to air.

Source Visibility

Tap shows or hides a source in a scene.

Virtual Camera

Tap starts or stops the OBS virtual camera. While it runs the icon glows blue.

Replay Buffer

Tap saves the last seconds (or starts the buffer). Hold stops it. Turn the buffer on in OBS: Settings, Output, Replay Buffer.

Screenshot

Tap saves a PNG of the program scene to your OBS recording folder.

Studio Mode

Tap turns Studio Mode on or off. Scene keys then show LIVE and NEXT.

Stats

OBS frame rate, CPU use and dropped frames, updated every 2 seconds.

OBS connection

In OBS: Tools, WebSocket Server Settings. Tick Enable WebSocket server, then Show Connect Info for the port and password. Shared by all OBS Deck keys.
